# Google Setup Checklist

This is the basic setup for Macabre & Cheese. Complete it in order.

## 1. Prepare now

- [ ] Choose the Google account that will permanently own the business profile.
- [ ] Keep the real business name written exactly as `Macabre & Cheese`.
- [ ] Make sure `https://macabreandcheese.com` is live.
- [ ] Get a public business phone number.
- [ ] Take clear photos of the truck, food, menu, and permanent truck signage.
- [ ] Decide which locations you actually serve before publishing any service area.
- [ ] Decide the real days and hours when customers can visit the truck.

Do not create a public location or service area before it is ready to be announced.

## 2. Create the Google Business Profile when the truck is operating

Google requires an eligible business to meet customers in person during its stated hours.

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/).
2. Sign in with the business owner's Google account.
3. Select **Add your business** or **Manage now**.
4. Enter the name exactly as `Macabre & Cheese`.
5. Choose **Food truck** as the primary category if Google offers it.
6. Add `https://macabreandcheese.com` as the website.
7. Add the public business phone number.
8. Add only real operating hours.
9. Add only locations or service areas where the truck is currently available.
10. Review everything, then continue to verification.

Do not add extra keywords such as city names or “best mac and cheese” to the business name.

## 3. Handle the address carefully

- If customers visit a permanent, staffed location with Macabre & Cheese signage, use its real address.
- If customers do not visit your business address, keep that address hidden from the public.
- Never use a P.O. box, mailbox, virtual office, or borrowed address.
- Add specific cities or ZIP codes only after you actually serve them.
- Keep the total service area realistic and close enough to operate.

## 4. Verify the Business Profile

Google chooses the available verification method. It may ask for a video.

For a verification video, be ready to show:

- The real truck and surrounding location.
- Macabre & Cheese branding or permanent signage.
- Food-truck equipment and business materials.
- Proof that you control the truck or business, such as unlocking it.

Follow the instructions shown by Google and submit the verification once.

## 5. Finish the profile after verification

- [ ] Upload the logo.
- [ ] Upload a clean cover photo.
- [ ] Add several real truck and food photos.
- [ ] Add the menu items and descriptions.
- [ ] Add an accurate business description.
- [ ] Confirm the website, phone, hours, and location information.
- [ ] Add opening updates and future truck stops as Google posts.

Suggested description after launch:

> Macabre & Cheese is a gothic food truck serving inventive mac and cheese, enchanted cheesecakes, and playful comfort food for the beautifully strange.

Do not put links, prices, sales, or promotional claims in the description.

## 6. Connect the website to Google Search Console

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/).
2. Select **Add property**.
3. Choose **Domain**.
4. Enter `macabreandcheese.com` without `https://` or a page path.
5. Copy the DNS verification record Google gives you.
6. Add that record at the company where the domain's DNS is managed.
7. Return to Search Console and select **Verify**.
8. Open **Sitemaps**.
9. Submit `https://macabreandcheese.com/sitemap.xml`.
10. Open **URL inspection**.
11. Inspect `https://macabreandcheese.com`.
12. Select **Request indexing**.

## 7. Ask for reviews after serving real customers

1. Open the verified Business Profile on a computer.
2. Select **Read reviews**.
3. Select **Get more reviews**.
4. Copy Google's review link or save the QR code.
5. Put the QR code on receipts, cards, or the truck.
6. Ask every customer for an honest review.
7. Reply politely and briefly to reviews.

Never offer free food, discounts, prizes, or other rewards for a review. Do not ask only happy customers, and do not ask anyone to write specific words.

## 8. Check it regularly

Once a week:

- [ ] Check that hours and truck stops are accurate.
- [ ] Reply to new reviews.
- [ ] Add a recent photo or useful update.
- [ ] Check Search Console for indexing or sitemap errors.

Update the website and Google profile together whenever the menu, hours, phone number, or operating area changes.
